# Changes waiting for the next app update

The desktop app gets an update once 10 changes have piled up here, unless Ryan asks for one sooner.
Add one line per change. `tools/publish_update.py` publishes them and empties this list.

- Match replays: every match you play is recorded. Watch it back from the result screen or the new Replays page, with step, previous/next turn, speed and a timeline.
- Choose the computer's difficulty: Easy, Normal or Hard.
- Cards that turn Life cards face-up or face-down now work, and face-up Life shows on the board.
- Abilities that make you rest your own cards or a DON!! to pay their cost now work.
- "Cannot be K.O.'d by effects" and "cannot be removed from the field" now protect the card.
- Cards that reveal the top card of your deck or Life now work: the card is shown to both players, then played, added to your hand or put back as the card says.
