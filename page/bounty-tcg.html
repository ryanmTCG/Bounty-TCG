Tips
  • Tap a card for its options, or drag it to a zone.
  • Tap a DON!! card to rest or activate it. Drag one onto your Leader or a Character to give it.
  • Drag the gold +N badge on a card to move that DON!! to another card, back to your cost area, or to the DON!! deck.
  • Drag a power or cost badge on a card onto another card to move it, or off the card to remove it.
  • Tap your Deck, Life, Trash or DON!! deck for actions.
  • In public and ranked matches, drawing, looking at your deck or Life, healing, DON!!, resting and moving cards by hand only work while a card's ability calls for them.
